Tilbud Radar – design options

Four directions shown on the same screen. Each one can use any of the five fonts – change the font under each title. Switch to dark mode to check that too, and open this page on your phone. Nothing here changes the live site until you pick one.

To choose, just say which option and font you like, e.g. “B with Inter”. Fonts are stored on the site itself (no Google Fonts requests) and all support æ, ø and å.
